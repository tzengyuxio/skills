# Mr. Chabuduo (Chinglish)

> 源文：胡適《差不多先生傳》（1924）
>
> 改寫風格：chinglish — 中式英語化(母語干擾下的英文系統性變異)
>
> 強度：Medium(直譯式結構 + 缺冠詞 / 時態混亂 / 量詞遺留)
>
> 看點：英文輸出但句法是中文 — 是 hanjify 的反面(那個是英文句法 + 漢字內容詞；這個是中文句法 + 英文詞彙)

---

You know China most famous person is who? His family name Cha, name Buduo,
each province each county each village all have him. Mr. Chabuduo's name
everyday all hang on everybody mouth, because he is whole China people's
representative.

He often say: "All thing only need chabuduo then okay, why must so careful?"

He at school time, teacher ask him: "Zhili province west side is which
province?" He say is Shaanxi. Teacher say: "Wrong, is Shanxi not Shaanxi."
He say: "Shaanxi and Shanxi, not chabuduo?"

When small time, his mother tell him go buy red sugar. He buy white sugar
come back. His mother scold him, he shake head say: "Red sugar white
sugar, not chabuduo?"

Later he get sick urgent. Family people panic, run to find East Street
Dr. Wang. One panic one run, East West two street, Wang and Wang cannot
separate, run to West Street cow doctor Wang's home. Cow doctor use cure-
cow method cure him. Mr. Chabuduo this way one life one breath finish.

Before die he still say: "Cure man cure cow, not chabuduo?"

He really is whole China representative.
